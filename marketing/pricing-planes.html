How it works

We come to you. Your hangar or tiedown, on Orcas or nearby.
You do not need to be there for the whole job, but be there at the start so you can point out anything you do not want touched.
Water is helpful, not required. We carry a waterless system for ramps without a hose.

Good to know

Book a week ahead where you can. Weather moves things around out here.
Ceramic needs dry hangar time to cure, usually overnight at least. We will say so before we start.
Aircraft only on this sheet. We do boats and cars too, just ask.
